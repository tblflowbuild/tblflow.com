The blank page is the first obstacle of any process map. TblFlow now offers a **library of template processes** that you import one by one into your process map.

## What's in it

13 processes, grouped by sector:

- **For any company**: customer invoicing, recruitment, complaint handling, purchasing and procurement;
- **Retail and e-commerce**, for example restocking and supplier orders, after-sales service and warranty, cycle counting;
- **Industry and logistics**, for example quality control and non-conformities, raw-material procurement, order picking and shipping;
- **Accounting and legal**, for example month-end close, monthly payroll, drafting and signing a contract.

Each process comes with its chained steps, sample durations and frequencies, and roles **without rates**. Nothing is written on the cost side: you enter your own values. Importing the same process twice does not create a duplicate.

## Validate step by step

Each step can now carry a **validation status** (to validate, validated, to review), the person who validated it, the date and a comment. A badge on the step, in the canvas, shows the state at a glance, and the detail appears on hover.

## What about existing maps?

They move to the updated model through the **"Update the model"** banner: TblFlow shows a preview of the changes and asks for confirmation before applying anything.

*The imported content is in French for now. The process map is included in the Pro, Business and Enterprise plans.*
