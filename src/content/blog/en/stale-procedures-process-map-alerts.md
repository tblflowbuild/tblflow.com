In most companies the procedure exists… and nobody knows when it was last reviewed. It ages in silence, until the day a newcomer follows it to the letter and ends up in front of a tool that no longer exists.

## One procedure sheet per process

In TblFlow's process map, every process now carries its own **procedure sheet**, written right in the canvas, next to the diagram of the steps. The process also has an **owner**, a **last review date**, a **next review date** and a **status**. The documentation and the diagram live in the same place, so they get fixed together.

## An email when a review is due

Once alerts are switched on from the canvas's "More actions" menu, TblFlow sends an email **every Monday at 7:00** (server time) listing the processes whose next review is **overdue or due within 14 days**. Archived processes are ignored. If there is nothing to review, **no email is sent**: no pointless noise.

You choose the recipients; by default it is the person who switches the alerts on. They can change them in the dialog provided for that.

## Getting started

1. Set a next review date on your most critical processes.
2. Switch on the staleness alerts.
3. When the email arrives, open the sheet, review it, then push the date back.

*Alerts require a V2 base, and the process map is included in the Pro, Business and Enterprise plans.*
