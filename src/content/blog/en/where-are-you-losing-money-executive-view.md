Management has no wish to open a 25-step canvas. They want to know: **where do we lose the most, and what are we doing about it?**

## The "Where are you losing money?" path

A read-only window ranks your **processes, then their steps**, by potential gain. For each one: its share of the total gain, its cost, its breaks, and a link to go straight to the step in the canvas.

## An executive view to print

The **executive view** fits on one page, readable on screen and **printable to PDF**:

- the cost and the potential gain;
- the **five processes** where you lose the most;
- the improvements, by status;
- the overdue reviews.

## A monthly report in your inbox

Switch on the **monthly report**: on the 1st of each month at 8:00 (server time), an email summarises the cost, the potential gain, the three processes with the biggest gain and the number of overdue reviews. No email when there is nothing to report.

## Amounts stay protected

Amounts are only shown to people who have the right to read the cost fields. For everyone else, TblFlow ranks by **number of breaks** and says so clearly, without showing a misleading zero. A monthly report with amounts can only be switched on by the base owner.

*Costs are entered estimates; the process map is included in the Pro, Business and Enterprise plans.*
