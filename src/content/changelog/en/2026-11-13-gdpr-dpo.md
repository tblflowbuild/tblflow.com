- **Field classification**: mark the fields that hold personal data.
- **DPO role**: a "Personal data" tab in the space settings brings together the audit view, the record of processing activities (Article 30) and the tracking of data-subject requests.
- **Log**: reads, edits and exports of tables with personal fields are logged, kept for 12 months.
- **Erasure**: erasing a person covers recent history and the archive.
