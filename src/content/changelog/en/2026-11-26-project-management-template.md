---
title: "A ready-to-use “Project management” template in the gallery"
date: 2026-11-26
translationKey: "project-management-template"
type: "feature"
---

- **A complete base to run your projects**: projects, tasks with subtasks and dependencies, and sprints.
- **Views already in place**: Kanban, Gantt with critical path, calendar and a progress dashboard.
- **Automatic end date**: it fills in when a task moves to “Done”.
- **An example to start from**: a small sample project shows the views filled in, to delete or adapt.
- **Two automations included**: turn them on in the Automations tab of your new base.
- **Works with the project manager assistant**: installing it on a base created from the template reuses its People, Projects and Tasks tables instead of creating new ones.
- **Languages**: the interface and the example are translated into English, other languages stay in French for now.
