---
title: "New “Tracked duration” field with a timer"
date: 2026-11-27
translationKey: "tracked-duration-field"
type: "feature"
---

- **A timer on every row**: add a “Tracked duration” field to a table. In a row’s record, a start / stop timer adds up the time spent, with the detail of each period per person.
- **Works everywhere**: the total shows in the grid, and can be filtered, sorted, summed and used in formulas. It appears as h:mm, or h:mm:ss with the “Show seconds” option.
- **Everyone has their own timer**: you can only delete your own periods, and a forgotten period is capped at 24 hours.
- **Grid, import, export**: putting the cell in edit mode (Enter) opens the row’s timer. CSV export uses h:mm:ss; import accepts “1:30”, “1h 30m”, “45m” or a number of seconds.
