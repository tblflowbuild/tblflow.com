---
title: "Account erasure: grace period and register"
date: 2026-11-02
translationKey: "account-erasure"
type: "improvement"
---

An account erasure request sent to TblFlow now follows a tooled procedure.

- **Grace period** of 30 days by default: sessions are cut off immediately, and the erasure can be cancelled during that period.
- **Register**: each step (scheduling, execution, cancellation) is recorded in a register, without the person's address.
- **What remains**: invoices and payments, which the law requires us to keep, and contributions in shared bases, displayed without an identifiable author.

You can still delete your own account yourself, with immediate effect.
