---

The document library now holds all your files, not just documents.

- **Media tab** next to documents: a grid with search by name and filters (images, audio, videos, documents, other files).
- **Drag and drop**: a Markdown file becomes a document, everything else goes to Media.
- **Private files**: new files are stored privately and read with your space's rights.
- **Previews**: images, audio and video display directly; PDF, Word and Excel open in a preview; a PowerPoint presentation shows the text of its slides.
- **Indexed text**: the text of PDF, Word and PowerPoint files is extracted and indexed, so search can find it.
- **In the editor**: the "/" command offers "Library media" to insert a file into a document.

Media storage depends on the plan: 200 MB on Free, 20 GB on Essential, 100 GB on Team, unlimited on Enterprise. See [pricing](/en/pricing).
