title: "Security: stronger isolation between spaces and between people"
date: 2026-11-22
translationKey: "security-isolation"

- **AI chat and agents isolated**: everyone sees only the data of their own space and their own conversations. An access refusal is now shown clearly.
- **Access cut immediately**: removing a member or lowering their role takes effect right away.
- **No remote images in the chat**: only the alt text is shown, so a booby-trapped answer cannot pass your data to a third party.
- **Independent customer spaces**: the org chart, real-time updates, plugins and branding no longer mix from one space to another.
- **Security updates**: Next.js moves to a version that fixes two known vulnerabilities, and other dependencies are updated.
