title: "AI fields and AI-generated media"
date: 2026-09-26
translationKey: "ai-media"

AI fields can now be switched off and show a count. A *Generate* button appears on each media field, to produce an image, a sound or a video from the table. Generation now follows the language of your request: a model, an interface or an automation described in Italian is written in Italian.
