title: "Driven browser: learning by demonstration"
date: 2026-09-20
translationKey: "browser"
type: "feature"
---

The driven browser (Computer Use) learns a skill by watching you work, not only by recording clicks. A row button can drive a website, replay a second authentication factor and bring the data back into the table. Automated replays are capped, and the screen tells you before it refuses one.
