title: "Settings: every page says what it does"
date: 2026-11-05
translationKey: "settings-help"

---

Fourteen settings pages now open with a "What it does" panel, followed by "Not to be confused with" and links to neighbouring pages when they do something similar. They include tokens, OAuth apps, connectors, permissions, collaborators, space AI, notifications and the Security and audit page.

The panels are translated into all 10 interface languages.
