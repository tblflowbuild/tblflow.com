- **AI credits per space**: TblFlow's AI offer runs on credits attached to the space, which you can top up.
- **Inference in Europe**: TblFlow AI goes through the European address of its inference provider. This choice is reflected in the privacy policy.
- **First screen with two paths**: when you first meet AI, you choose between your own key and TblFlow AI, with a list of first steps.
- **Clear error**: when the AI provider refuses the call, TblFlow shows the error instead of an empty answer.

See [pricing](/en/pricing) for the detail of the AI offer.
