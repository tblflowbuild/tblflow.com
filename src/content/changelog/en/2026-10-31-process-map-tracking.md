The process map (called "Carto des process" in the French interface) no longer just draws: it now measures, tracks over time and helps you steer. Costs remain **estimates that you enter**, not measurements. The process map is available on the Pro, Business and Enterprise plans only.

**Track over time**

- **Procedure sheet** on every process (Canvas field), with owner, review dates and status.
- **Staleness alerts**: an email every Monday at 7:00 (server time) lists the processes whose next review is overdue or due within 14 days. No email if there is nothing to review. Recipients can be changed; switch it on from the canvas's "More actions" menu. A V2 base is required.
- **Monthly report by email**: on the 1st of the month at 8:00, cost, potential gain, the three processes with the biggest gain and the overdue reviews. Amounts only appear if the base owner switches them on. A V2 base is required.
- **Per-step validation**: status (to validate, validated, to review), validated by, date and comment, with a badge on the step in the canvas.

**Measure and compare**

- **Estimated vs actual**: enter measurements on steps (minutes, volume, error rate, source); the panel compares the estimate with the measurement and shows the share of measured steps.
- **Before / after**: freeze the map in a copy, then compare the total cost for the month and the number of steps.
- **Improvement register**: track your ideas by status, with linked steps, cost of implementation and targeted gain.

**Steer**

- **"Where are you losing money?" path**: a ranking of processes, then steps, by potential gain.
- **Executive view**: a read-only page, printable to PDF (cost, potential gain, the five processes where you lose the most, improvements by status, overdue reviews).

**Start faster**

- **Template process library**: 13 processes ready to import one at a time (customer invoicing, recruitment, complaints, purchasing, plus series for retail, industry and logistics, and accounting and legal), grouped by sector.
- **Model upgrade**: existing maps move to the updated model through the "Update the model" banner, with a preview of the changes and a confirmation.

**Security**: the public link is closed by default; amounts and cost fields are hidden from anyone who is not allowed to read them; `.tbl` export and duplication are refused if a field is unreadable for the caller.
