---

- **“AI page” in the Canvas field**: AI writes a structured page, a prospect sheet or a presentation, from the row’s fields. You can add optional instructions.
- **Two views**: “Page”, a formatted sheet (or a slideshow when the presentation has several slides, with previous / next), and “Edit”.
- **Batch generation**: for the selection, or for rows that have no page yet, up to 100 per run. Each page counts toward AI usage, and the batch stops if the quota is reached.

This is the first step of this work.
