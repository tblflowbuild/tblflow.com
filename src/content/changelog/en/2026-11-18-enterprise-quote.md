- **Proposal**: on the Enterprise plan (on-premise or your own cloud), TblFlow sends you a priced proposal as a PDF.
- **Online acceptance**: the space owner accepts the proposal online.
- **Payment**: once the proposal is accepted, you pay by card or SEPA direct debit.
- **Request**: the “Request a proposal” button on the pricing page starts the process. See [pricing](/en/pricing).
