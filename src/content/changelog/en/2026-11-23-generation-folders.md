- **Applications ready for your trade**: when the AI creates your application, it computes totals and counts across tables (amount invoiced per customer, hours per project), links a table to itself for hierarchies, makes essential fields required, and adds calendar and Gantt views to dated tables. Every amount keeps its currency.
- **Views and amounts in your language**: views created by the AI are named in your language, and amounts display your way (“1,234.50” in English, “1 234,50 €” in French).
- **Folders**: organize the bases of a space into folders (two levels) by drag and drop. Inside a base, the List view shows the same folders to organize tables, interfaces, automations, agents and whiteboards.
- **“Generate with AI”**: describe a table, a field or a button in one sentence; a preview appears and nothing is created until you click “Create”.
- **Guided onboarding**: account, AI, your website address (TblFlow prepares your brand and your design.md, which you review), then a checklist of steps you can skip.
