The process map is available on the Pro plan and above. It is a page of your space: a canvas to draw who does what (a person, an automation or an AI agent), read four ways: *Who does what*, *Costs*, *Breaks* and *Data*.

It spots re-keying between tools, data with no master source, steps with no owner or no next step, and failing automations. Costs are estimates that you enter, not measurements. On a step or a tool, *Replace* asks the AI to propose a TblFlow alternative; everything it creates is inactive, and you switch it on. The map sits on real PostgreSQL tables in your space and can be shared read-only through a link.

Full overview: [/en/process-map](/en/process-map). Step-by-step guide: [documentation](/en/docs/process-map).
