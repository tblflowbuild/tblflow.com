The site and its language switcher now support German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Russian, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Chinese — a flag dropdown in the header and footer, with clean per-language routing on every page.

Product copy (interface, pricing, comparisons) is catching up to this list as translation work continues; pages not yet translated show in English rather than erroring out.
