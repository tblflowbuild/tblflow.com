---
title: "Free viewers, with a cap that depends on your plan"
date: 2026-11-27
translationKey: "free-viewers"
type: "feature"
---

- **Viewers without a paid seat**: people invited to read or comment don’t take a paid seat, up to a cap that depends on your plan.
- **One shared cap**: both roles use the same counter. Beyond it, each viewer takes a paid seat, or the invitation is refused if the workspace is full.
- **The counter is visible**: “Free viewers: x / y” in the workspace, and “unlimited” when the plan has no cap.
