date: 2026-11-18

A new type of resource in a base: a canvas to sketch and think together, with AI.

- **Creating one**: "New whiteboard" button in the space, with six starter templates. The whiteboard inherits the base's permissions, goes through the trash and follows the base when it is copied.
- **Editing**: frames, typed sticky notes, text, shapes, links, images, pen drawing and comments. Undo, copy and paste, minimap, snap-to-grid and presentation mode.
- **Together**: real-time editing, collaborators' cursors, dot voting, private mode, timer and tree layout.
- **With your data**: drop a record onto the canvas, turn a selection into records or a diagram into tables, send the result to the process map.
- **With AI**: the assistant reads the open whiteboard and proposes adding, linking, grouping or moving items; you approve a preview before anything changes. Quick actions: summarise, group by theme, generate.
- **Export**: as PNG or PDF.
