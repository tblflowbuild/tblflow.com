- **Connectors lens**: it shows the flows between your tools, how each one is transferred and which catalog connector to use for it.
- **A lens that comes alive**: it tells the story of the handovers between steps and offers a “what if” simulation.
- **Step panel on the right**: it opens next to the map, with a summary of the process.
- **The AI chat reads and edits the process map**.
