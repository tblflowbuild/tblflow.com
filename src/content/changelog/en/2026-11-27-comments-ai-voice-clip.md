date: 2026-11-27

---

- **@AI in a comment**: type “@” then choose “AI”. The base assistant reads the record and replies right in the thread.
- **Voice message and screen clip**: attach either from the comment field. A voice message lasts up to 5 minutes, a screen clip up to 3 minutes; your browser asks for microphone or screen access.
- **Your rules still apply**: the assistant respects your permissions, your plan’s AI quota and the objection to AI processing. Any write it proposes goes through your approval.
- Recordings are stored like comment images: same permissions, same storage quota.
