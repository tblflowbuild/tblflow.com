title: "Project manager assistant: tables, RACI and a prepared morning"
date: 2026-11-23
translationKey: "project-assistant"
type: "feature"
---

- **One-click setup**: from the “+” menu of a base, the assistant installs the People, Projects (with a RACI per deliverable) and Tasks tables.
- **An agent that prepares your mornings**: every weekday morning it reads Google Calendar and Gmail read-only, drops the meeting agenda into the project record, proposes work blocks in your calendar and flags tasks without an owner.
- **Nothing without your approval**: every write goes through an approval, and the agent never sends an email.
- **Safe to reinstall**: running the setup again refreshes the agent without touching your existing tables.
