A new AI Act tab in the space settings helps you prepare your obligations under the European AI regulation. It does not replace legal advice.

- **Inventory**: computed from the space's objects (AI fields, agents, automations with an AI step, MCP, document search, chat).
- **Purpose questionnaire**: the decision stays human. A use goes back to "to review" when it changes.
- **Design analysis**: it flags gaps (personal-data fields, human validation, logging). These are warnings; nothing is blocked.
- **When creating an agent**: a short questionnaire asks for its purpose.
- **Transparency**: a public sheet describes each of TblFlow's AI functions.
