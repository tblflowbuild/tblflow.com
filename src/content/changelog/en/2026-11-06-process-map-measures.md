- **Import measurements from the automation**: on a step run by an automation, a button computes the average duration, monthly volume and failure rate from the last 30 days of runs. You see the before and after, and nothing is written until you confirm.
- **Industry vocabulary**: five presets adapt the words "customer" and "order" to your trade (services and consulting, retail, industry and logistics, accounting and legal, healthcare). Tables and fields are not renamed.
- **Free diagnosis**: the Free plan lets you map one process and see its estimated costs. Replace, the monthly report and estimated vs actual stay visible but require the Essential plan.

Guide: [Process map](/en/docs/process-map).
