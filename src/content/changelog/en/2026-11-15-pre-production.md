title: "Pre-production space, with an anonymised copy"
date: 2026-11-15
translationKey: "preprod"

---

Test a solution in a separate space before putting it live.

- **Creating one**: a pre-production is a space linked to your production space. It is reserved for the Team and Enterprise plans and for the space owner, and does not take up a seat.
- **Anonymised copy**: the structure and rows are copied. Personal-data fields are replaced with consistent, non-reversible fictitious values. Production identifiers of automations and agents are never copied.
- **Cues**: permanent banner, emails prefixed "[Pre-prod]", tester invitations.
- **Publishing**: a preview of the differences comes first. An item modified on both sides is a conflict and stays blocked; the rest is published. No data is ever published, and deletions are flagged without being applied.
- **Refresh**: on confirmation, from production.
- **AI credits**: the pre-production draws on the linked production space's AI credits.
