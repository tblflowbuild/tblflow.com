Arriving in TblFlow has been redesigned to get you to something useful quickly.

- **AI first**: paste your provider's key, it is tested on the spot and the models are chosen for you. A three-step guide helps you get a key. You can also skip and come back later.
- **Your company in a few questions**: departments, tools and, if you wish, your existing procedures (PDF, Markdown, text or Word) dropped into the document library.
- **A pre-filled process map** with the departments and tools you declared, then a first generated app.
- **A "Getting started" checklist** to finish setup: AI, brand, org chart, notifications, web search, process map, first app, procedures. It hides itself when you no longer need it.

A member invited to an existing space skips these steps and lands directly in the space.
