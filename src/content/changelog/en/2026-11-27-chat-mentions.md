---
title: "AI chat: mention a table, a view or a field with @"
date: 2026-11-27
translationKey: "chat-mentions"
type: "feature"
---

- **Type “@”** in the AI chat: a searchable list offers your tables, views and fields, grouped by type.
- **A precise mention**: the object you pick appears as a chip with its type (table, view or field) and its name, so the assistant knows exactly what you are talking about.
- **Based on your permissions**: if a mention isn’t accessible, a message invites you to remove it or pick an object from the list.
