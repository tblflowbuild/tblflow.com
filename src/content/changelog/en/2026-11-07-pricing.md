Plans change shape: one price per team, with people included, then extra seats billed individually. "Pro" and "Business" become **Essential** and **Team**.

- **Free**, €0: 2 people, with the **free diagnosis** of the process map (1 process, costs visible, without the Replace button or the monthly report).
- **Essential**, €39 per month: 5 people included, then €7 per additional person.
- **Team**, €119 per month: 15 people included, then €6 per additional person.
- **Enterprise**, from €399 per month: 50 people included, then €5 per additional person. By quote beyond 50 people or for self-hosting.
- **Annual**: 2 months free on every paid plan.

AI features still use your own provider key; a step-by-step guide helps you set it up. Plan details: [pricing](/en/pricing).
