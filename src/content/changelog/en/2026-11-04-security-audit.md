An internal security audit carried out on 2 November 2026 led to a series of fixes, delivered between 3 and 7 November.

- **Application pages**: hardened content security policy, with no dynamic code evaluation.
- **Isolated previews and extensions**: the app preview and extensions run in isolated frames.
- **Space AI providers**: a guard rejects addresses that point to the server's internal network.
- **Field rights**: a field you cannot read no longer shows through a sort, a grouping, a calendar or a table duplication.
- **Sessions, data and files**: tighter access controls.

We do not detail the flaws that were fixed.
